Why This Matters: What Life Looks Like at Each Level
Grade 0-1
Cannot read a medicine bottle label, a stop sign, or their child's report card. Cannot write their own name on a form.
Grade 2-3
Can read simple sentences but struggles to fill out a job application, understand a lease, or follow written instructions at work.
Grade 4-5
Can read basic documents but has difficulty with math needed to manage a household budget, calculate a tip, or compare prices.
Grade 6-8
Approaching workplace readiness. Can handle most reading tasks but may struggle with technical manuals, workplace safety documents, or college applications.
Grade 9-12
Near high-school level. Preparing for HSE testing or direct college enrollment. Ready for career training.
ESL
Learning English as a new language. May be highly educated in their home country but need English literacy and workplace communication skills.

Does it work?
Adult Education WORKS
2 out of 3
high school equivalency diplomas earned in Louisiana come from adult learners served through LCTC's Adult Education network
The Problem We're Solving
Across Louisiana, the statewide pass rate for HSE testing is declining — from 72.3% to 58.0% over three years. More people are attempting the HiSET or GED on their own, and most are not passing. The number of HiSET and GED test takers who didn't pass all sections attempted within the same year has nearly doubled (+96%), and they may not know that testing isn't the only way to earn an HSE in Louisiana!
Meanwhile, adult learners served through LCTC's Adult Education network are succeeding at increasing rates — 9.0% to 11.9%, and now earn 68.3% of all diplomas statewide, up from 65.9%. The difference is instruction, support, and a teacher who cares.
It is hard to prepare on your own with no instruction and no one to keep you motivated. That is why Adult Education exists — and why investment in outreach has never been more important. Find your local program →
From classroom to career
Industry credentials: Real skills for real Louisiana jobs
In FY 2025-26, 1,564 adult learners served through LCTC's Adult Education network earned 1,995 industry-recognized credentials — certifications that Louisiana employers hire for. Here are some of the most common:
Basic Life Support (BLS) / CPR & First Aid
Healthcare: hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, emergency services
NCCER Core Curriculum
Construction: welding, electrical, pipefitting, instrumentation — entry to Registered Apprenticeship
Certified Nurse Aide (CNA)
Healthcare: direct patient care in hospitals and long-term care — pathway to LPN and RN
Forklift Operator Certification
Manufacturing & logistics: warehouses, distribution centers, industrial plants
Conover Workplace Readiness
All industries: professional communication, teamwork, problem-solving — the skills employers say are hardest to find
OSHA 10 Safety Certification
Construction & manufacturing: required safety credential for job sites across Louisiana
ServSafe Food Handler
Food service & hospitality: restaurants, catering, institutional food service
NCCER Welding / Electrical Level 1
Skilled trades: journeyman pathway, $20-35/hr starting wages in Louisiana
Each credential is a step on a career pathway. Entry-level credentials lead to higher-rated, higher-paying certifications. Louisiana's LA Works system rates credentials 1-5 stars by employer demand and wage outcomes. Learn more about Adult Career Pathways →
